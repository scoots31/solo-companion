Consistency score uses weighted rolling average
Design Review Apr 14
Scoring algorithm uses a 6-game weighted rolling average rather than a season-long mean. Recent games are weighted 2× relative to earlier games.
Why: Season-long means over-reward players who had a hot start but declined. Recent form is more predictive of next week's performance.
Partial indicator for players with fewer than 6 games
Design Sprint Apr 11
Rookies and players returning from injury with fewer than 6 games of data show a partial indicator rather than a full score or an error state.
Why: An error state implies the product is broken. A partial indicator communicates the data limitation honestly while still being useful.

Discovery Brief — player-evaluation

Date: 2026-04-05  ·  Status: Complete

The Story

The solo builder needs a fast, reliable signal on player consistency for dynasty fantasy football decisions. Today that means manually pulling game logs, running calculations in a spreadsheet, and making judgment calls without a standardized measure. The evaluation is slow, inconsistent, and impossible to scale across a roster.

The player evaluation tool removes that ceiling. It calculates a consistency score for any player in the MFL database using a weighted rolling average of recent game performance — giving dynasty managers a single reliable number they can act on immediately.

Key Moments

  • Player lookup — Solo searches by name, selects the player, sees the consistency score and supporting game log data instantly.
  • Roster scan — Solo evaluates multiple players in sequence to compare consistency across a position group before a trade.
  • Rookie assessment — Player with fewer than 6 games shows a partial indicator rather than a full score, communicating data limitation honestly.

Open Threads

  • Playoff weighting — deferred to Phase 2. Regular season and playoffs treated equally in Phase 1.
  • Export / share — not in scope for Phase 1.

What We're Not Building Yet

  • Comparison view — side-by-side player scoring. Phase 2.
  • League-wide roster scan — requires additional MFL API work. Phase 2.
